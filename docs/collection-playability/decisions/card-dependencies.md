# Card-level dependency scope

The gate detects text about draw restrictions, mana ability activation, prevention, replacement, activated ability costs, spell casting and timing or frequency restrictions. Explicit draw-only and mana-ability-only restrictions are scoped to those functions. Other modifiers whose affected object cannot be established are treated as potentially affecting all other uses on the card. Keyword-only and unrecognized static or ambiguous text receive unknown status. Dependency assessments are limited to the printed Oracle snapshot and are not proof of rules independence.

Explicit rule-modifying clauses are also considered when they appear in activated, triggered or spell boundaries. Those dependencies carry conditional timing because their effect may require resolution. The gate records same-face, cross-face, and mixed relationships. Face state and effect persistence are not simulated, so cross-face effects that could apply remain uncertain. Flying, vigilance, haste and trample in standalone keyword boundaries are treated as unrelated to the supported draw, mana and interaction shapes. Other keyword text remains unknown.

The detector uses bounded textual patterns and native capability groups. A modifier word inside a triggered or activated boundary can be detected, but an unrecognized wording or a rule imposed by another permanent may still be missed. Unknown scope can affect all identified uses, including uses that are actually independent. Standalone card-advantage fallbacks remain unknown. `independent` describes the current detection scope rather than complete rules independence.
