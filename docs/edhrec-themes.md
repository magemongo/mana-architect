# EDHREC Theme Coverage

Snapshot retrieved on November 14, 2026 for catalog 1.5.0. EDHREC tags can overlap, and their deck counts do not affect Mana Architect's scores. [EDHREC Theme Tags](https://edhrec.com/tags/themes)

**Scored** entries have a selectable strategy in both ranking paths. The descriptions in `config/strategy_catalog.json` explain the plan, and the capability preferences in `config/strategy_synergy.json` describe the evidence currently recognized. Support for a theme can start with its signature mechanic or card type. Some enablers and payoffs still require parser coverage.

**Reference** entries are available here for planning future catalog work. Broad labels, companion restrictions, named-card shells, and alternate formats often need deck-level or card-specific evidence. X Spells and Exile are examples of popular themes that would benefit from more specific evidence.
